# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese-first solo operators, small teams, consultants, accountants, and analysts who work with spreadsheets, recurring exports, folders, or receipts and need a dependable dashboard without writing code.

## Product Purpose

DataBreeze turns user-controlled files, folders, receipts, and datasets into governed, interactive dashboards and evidence-backed analysis. The experience should make the next useful action obvious while showing where data lives, what changed, how quality was measured, and why a result can be trusted.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, and exact. The product should feel premium and capable without becoming flashy: a clear Vietnamese-first voice, blue-led visual identity, strong information hierarchy, and visible evidence behind automation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic green “success dashboard” styling, grey enterprise forms, neon AI gimmicks, opaque magic, fake-looking metrics, decorative motion that competes with the task, and interfaces that hide rejected data, freshness, cost, or uncertainty.

## Design Principles

- Trust before magic: explain material outputs and preserve evidence.
- Make the next useful action obvious: onboarding begins with adding data and reaching a useful result.
- Keep the blue DataBreeze identity consistent from landing page through authentication and workspace.
- Use business language first; expose technical detail progressively when it helps a decision.
- Keep public marketing expressive, but keep authenticated workflows calm, dense, and operational.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA on Web and equivalent native expectations on Desktop and Android. Maintain Vietnamese as the default locale with complete English parity, keyboard and screen-reader support, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, responsive layouts, and explicit loading, empty, error, disabled, and unavailable states.
